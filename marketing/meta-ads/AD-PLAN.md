# La La Land Nail Hub: Meta ad plan

**Campaign name:** Now Booking in Scarborough

**In one line:** Show Bianca's real work to women near Scarborough on Instagram and Facebook, and send them to lalalandnails.ca to request a weekday appointment.

## The goal

- **Main goal:** appointment requests from new clients.
- **What full looks like:** Bianca takes one client per weekday, so about 21 appointments a month. The ads only need to fill those.
- **First-month target:** 8 to 12 requests from ads. This is a starting guess, not a promise. Week 2 results will show what is realistic.

## Budget

Bianca can only take about 21 clients a month, so a small budget is enough.

| Monthly budget | Per day | Good for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **$300 (recommended start)** | $10 | Testing 5 reels and learning which ones bring requests |
| $500 | $16 | Faster learning, or a second round of reels in the same month |
| $1,000 | $33 | More than one person's calendar can use right now |

Start at $300. Raise it only if requests are coming in and there are still open days.

## Who sees the ads

- **Where:** within about 15 km of Scarborough. This reaches east Toronto, Pickering and Ajax.
- **Who:** women aged 21 to 55.
- **Interests:** leave open. Meta finds the right people faster when it is not boxed in.
- **Placements:** Instagram and Facebook Reels and Stories. All 16 reels are made in that vertical shape.

## Which reels to run

**Round 1 (weeks 1 and 2): five reels with five different angles**

| Reel | Angle |
| --- | --- |
| 1 Now booking | The launch news |
| 2 Before and after | Proof of the work |
| 3 Meet Bianca | The person behind it |
| 4 Question hook | "Looking for a nail tech in Scarborough?" |
| 11 Prices | Clear prices up front |

**Round 2 (weeks 3 and 4):** turn off the two weakest. Add two of these:

- 8 One client a day
- 14 Weekdays
- 10 How booking works
- 5 Soft gel tips
- 6 Structured gel
- 12 Classic red

**Stories only:** the short cuts (9, 15, 16) work well as quick reminders once round 1 has run.

## Ad wording

Use one of these as the text above the video. They are written as Bianca. She should edit them so they sound like her.

1. Hi, I'm Bianca. I've opened La La Land Nail Hub in Scarborough and I'm taking new clients. European manicures from $45, gel from $60. I see one client a day, Monday to Friday. Request your time on my site.
2. Looking for a nail tech in Scarborough? I do European manicures, structured gel and soft gel tips. Pick a weekday time, choose your service, and I'll confirm the price and details with you.
3. One client a day, so your appointment is never rushed. European manicures from $45 in Scarborough. Request a weekday time at lalalandnails.ca.

- **Headline:** Now booking in Scarborough
- **Button:** Book now
- **Link:** `https://lalalandnails.ca/?utm_source=instagram`

The `utm_source=instagram` part makes each request from the ads show up labelled "instagram" in the staff workspace, so they can be counted.

## Setup in Meta Ads Manager

1. Create a campaign. Choose **Traffic** as the goal.
2. Budget: $10 a day. Location: Scarborough plus 15 km. Women, 21 to 55.
3. Placements: Reels and Stories on Instagram and Facebook.
4. Add the five round 1 reels as five ads in the same ad set. Use the matching cover image for each.
5. Add music from Meta's free library. The reels have no sound.
6. Paste the wording, headline, button and link from above.

## Four-week calendar

| Week | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Before launch | Bianca approves captions and wording. Facebook Page, Instagram account and ad account are ready. |
| 1 | Launch round 1. Do not change anything for 7 days. |
| 2 | Check results. Note the two weakest reels. |
| 3 | Swap the two weakest for two round 2 reels. |
| 4 | Check results. Decide next month's budget. Add any new clips Bianca has filmed. |

## How to tell if it is working

Check once a week.

| What to look at | Where | Good sign |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Requests labelled "instagram"** | Staff workspace on the site | Any at all in week 1, then steady |
| Cost per request | Ad spend divided by requests | Well under the price of one visit |
| Link clicks | Meta Ads Manager | About 1 click per 100 views or better |
| People watching past 3 seconds | Meta Ads Manager | The best reels stand out clearly |

Pause the ads whenever the calendar is full for the next two weeks.

## Risks

- **Meta cannot see bookings yet.** The site has no Meta tracking code (the "Pixel"), so Meta aims for clicks, not requests. Adding the Pixel later would let it aim for requests. That needs a change to the site and the privacy page.
- **The reels will get stale.** All 16 use the same 3 videos and 13 photos. A few new phone clips from Bianca each month would fix that.
- **The exact address is private.** The site shows Scarborough only. Some people will ask where the studio is before they book, so Bianca should have a ready answer.
